# AI Automation Specialist (Remote)

**Company:** BrightOps Solutions
**Location:** Remote (US-based)
**Type:** Full-time, hourly
**Pay:** $18–25/hr depending on experience

## About Us

BrightOps Solutions helps small and mid-size businesses streamline their back-office operations with AI-powered workflows. We're a fully remote team of 12 building automation pipelines for clients in e-commerce, logistics, and professional services.

## The Role

We're looking for an AI Automation Specialist to design, build, and maintain automated workflows for our clients. You'll work with LLM tools (we primarily use Claude), Python scripts, and no-code platforms to turn repetitive manual processes — data entry, report generation, file management, content production — into reliable automated pipelines.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain AI-driven automation workflows using Claude (API, Skills, agent-based tooling) and Python
- Design structured data pipelines: ingest messy client data (CSV, spreadsheets, documents), clean and normalize it, and generate accurate reports
- Write clear prompts and reusable skill templates that produce consistent, brand-aligned output
- Document workflows so non-technical clients and teammates can run and maintain them
- Test and QA automation outputs for accuracy before client delivery
- Communicate progress async via Slack and weekly video check-ins

## Required Qualifications

- Hands-on experience building automations with LLMs (Claude preferred) — prompt engineering, structured JSON outputs, multi-step workflows
- Working knowledge of Python for scripting and data processing
- Strong attention to detail and a process-optimization mindset
- Self-directed: able to manage your own queue and deliver reliably in a remote, async environment
- Clear written communication

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with Claude Skills, subagents, MCP, or the Claude API
- Portfolio of automation projects (GitHub or similar)
- Background in operations, logistics, or data-heavy environments
- Familiarity with Git/GitHub workflows
- Experience writing documentation or SOPs

## What We Offer

- $18–25/hr, paid weekly
- Fully remote, flexible schedule (core overlap 11am–3pm ET)
- Growth path to Senior Automation Engineer
- Equipment stipend and paid training time

## How to Apply

Send your resume and a link to an automation project you've built. A short cover note about why this role fits you goes a long way.
